# QuestBridge XR — smooth recessed-LED headboard

This update changes **only the existing headboard**. The diamond-tufted details are gone; the bed uses one continuous charcoal upholstered panel with a modestly softened edge. A stepped inner bezel and outer frame lip extend in front of a recessed light channel.

## Headboard lighting

- The upholstered center is a single uninterrupted panel with a subtle edge radius, not a heavily rounded cushion.
- The inner bezel and outer frame extend slightly beyond the light channel. This shades the emitter from reclined and oblique viewing angles.
- The colored light bounce remains active around the headboard. The thin emitter line is only revealed when looking nearly straight at the headboard.
- The old six-button bedside remote is removed. All lighting is controlled from **Environment → LED Colors** in the monitor menu.
- Seven independent zones are available: headboard glow, ceiling cove, left wall, right wall, rear wall, left bedside lamp, and right bedside lamp. Each zone has its own power, brightness, solid color or two-color gradient, and color-wheel pickers.

## What stays exactly as it was

- The bed platform, rails, and foot end. No new or redesigned footboard is added.
- The mattress, fitted sheet, original sleep pillows, accent cushions, duvet and throw.
- The bed's position and anchor in the room, its collision footprint, bedside furniture, TV, monitor, window, and other room objects.
- The existing head-tracking, turning, locomotion, and wall light-switch interaction code.

## Reference direction

- [4 EVER WINNER LED bed frame at Walmart](https://www.walmart.com/ip/4-EVER-WINNER-Queen-Size-LED-Bed-Frame-with-Headboard-Upholstered-Platform-Bed-Frame-with-LED-Lights-Diamond-Tufted-Headboard-and-Footboard-Black) — the headboard is adapted as a smooth panel with hidden, indirect lighting rather than copying the diamond tufting.

No third-party 3D bed mesh is used.

## Blender project

`arc_upholstered_bed.blend` is generated by the Blender 4.5.15 workflow from `arc_upholstered_bed_editable.glb`. The model remains in the editable bed collection, while the studio floor, area lights and reviewer cameras are kept in a separate render-only collection. Fabric images are packed into the Blend file.

To regenerate the mesh from the repository root:

```bash
QBX_BED_OUTPUT_DIR=art/bed \
QBX_BED_RUNTIME_GLB=quest-client/app/src/main/assets/models/arc_upholstered_bed.glb \
python tools/generate_bed_asset.py
```

Then, with Blender 4.5.15 or newer installed:

```bash
blender --background --python tools/blender_import_bed.py -- \
  --source art/bed/arc_upholstered_bed_editable.glb \
  --output art/bed/arc_upholstered_bed.blend \
  --render-dir art/bed/renders
```
