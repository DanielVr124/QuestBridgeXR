# QuestBridge XR — recessed, individually controllable LED headboard

This update changes **only the existing headboard** and the lighting controls. The face is one continuous, smooth charcoal upholstered panel with a restrained rounded edge. Inner upholstered bezel lips and an outer satin-silver cap project forward beyond the lighting channel, hiding the emitter from a low reclined angle while leaving a narrow sightline when viewed directly.

## Headboard construction

- One uninterrupted upholstered panel; no diamond tufting or buttons.
- Soft, moderate edge rounding rather than a heavily pill-shaped cushion.
- Inner and outer bezel rails extend forward past the recessed lighting channel.
- No raised, exposed LED strip or diffuser mesh. The renderer supplies a recessed, colorable glow behind the lips, plus a subtle light bounce on nearby surfaces.
- The original headboard wings and mounting brackets remain.

## Individual lighting controls

The Environment menu includes a Lighting page with separate zones for the headboard, ceiling cove, left and right wall accents, rear accent, and left and right bedside lamps. Each zone can be enabled independently, adjusted for brightness, and set to a solid color or a two-color gradient. Both gradient endpoints use the same HSV color-wheel picker.

## What stays exactly as it was

- The bed platform, rails, and foot end. No new or redesigned footboard is added.
- The mattress, fitted sheet, original sleep pillows, accent cushions, duvet and throw.
- The bed's position and anchor in the room, its collision footprint, bedside furniture, TV, monitor, window, and other room objects.
- The existing head-tracking, turning, locomotion and room movement code.

## Blender project

`arc_upholstered_bed.blend` is generated by the Blender 4.5.15 workflow from `arc_upholstered_bed_editable.glb`. The model remains in the editable bed collection, while the studio floor, area lights and reviewer cameras are kept in a separate render-only collection.

To regenerate the mesh from the repository root:

```bash
QBX_BED_OUTPUT_DIR=art/bed \
QBX_BED_RUNTIME_GLB=quest-client/app/src/main/assets/models/arc_upholstered_bed.glb \
python tools/generate_bed_asset.py
```
